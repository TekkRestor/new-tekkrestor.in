Privacy Policy

Last reviewed: 20 October 2026

Information we collect

When you contact TekkRestor or raise a support ticket, we may collect the information you submit, such as your name, contact details, organization, service request, device details and attachments.

How we use information

We use submitted information to respond to enquiries, assess service requests, provide support, communicate ticket updates, prevent abuse, maintain service records and meet applicable legal obligations.

Support tickets and attachments

Do not submit passwords, payment card data, government identity numbers or information that is not needed to diagnose the issue. Attachments may contain technical or business information; submit them only when necessary and with permission.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share the minimum necessary information with hosting, email, IT service or downstream recycling partners when needed to provide the requested service, subject to applicable requirements.

Retention and security

We retain information only for as long as needed for support, business records, security, dispute handling and legal requirements. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Before production, the site owner must configure a documented retention schedule, secure backups, access controls and a process for handling deletion or access requests.

Your choices and requests

To ask about, correct or request deletion of information you submitted, contact contact@tekkrestor.in. We may need to verify your identity and may retain records where legally or operationally necessary.

Cookies and analytics

This static site does not intentionally set advertising cookies by default. If analytics, advertising or embedded third-party services are enabled later, update this policy and any required consent controls to describe them accurately.
